Welcome to this session on growth strategy. When thinking about sustainable scaling, growth is never just about top-of-funnel customer acquisition. True durable growth is anchored in retention and monetization.

## 1. Why Retention is the Bedrock

If your retention curves are trending down toward zero, pouring more ad spend or marketing budget into user acquisition is like pouring water into a leaky bucket. You must first establish a stable cohort baseline. 

A healthy product will see its cohort retention curve flatten out over time—whether measured at day 30, week 12, or month 6. That flat horizontal tail represents your core engaged user base who find ongoing, recurring value in your core product loops.

## 2. The Growth Accounting Equation

Growth accounting decomposes your active user changes between two consecutive periods into explicit buckets:

- New Users: First-time users joining during the period.
- Resurrected Users: Previously churned users who returned.
- Retained Users: Existing users from the prior period who stayed active.
- Churned Users: Users active in the previous period who failed to return.

Net Active User Growth equals (New + Resurrected) minus Churned.

## 3. Product-Led Loops vs. Sales-Assisted Growth

Product-led growth (PLG) relies on the software itself to drive onboarding, activation, and expansion. Rather than waiting for a sales call, users experience the aha moment independently within the product. 

However, as contract values expand into the enterprise tier, combining product-led self-serve with targeted sales assistance creates the most resilient enterprise motion.
